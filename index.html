The problem

Two crises, one overlooked fix

01

Organic waste keeps piling up

Markets, farms, and homes generate large volumes of organic waste with no structured way to process it, so much of it is burned or dumped.

02

Power is unreliable and costly

Grid electricity is inconsistent and generator fuel is expensive, leaving households and small businesses to absorb the cost of constant outages.

03

The two problems can solve each other

Biogas digestion turns the waste problem into the power solution — but the equipment, setup, and site work need funding to get started.

How it works

From bin to breaker box

Collect organic waste

Food scraps, farm residue, and other organic matter are gathered from the surrounding community instead of being dumped or burned.

Break it down in the digester

The waste ferments inside a sealed digester tank, where bacteria break it down and release biogas as a byproduct.

Convert gas into electricity

The captured biogas fuels a generator that produces electricity, which is routed to homes, businesses, or the local grid.
